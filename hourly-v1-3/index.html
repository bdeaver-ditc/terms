HOURLY SERVICE TERMS

DeaverCo, LLC d/b/a Deaver IT Consulting

Version 1.3 · Effective November 23, 2026

1. Parties and acceptance. These terms govern services performed by DeaverCo, LLC d/b/a Deaver IT Consulting ("Deaver IT") for a client ("Client") that does not have a Master Services Agreement with Deaver IT. Before Deaver IT first performs work for Client, Deaver IT sends Client a written work authorization request that describes the initial request, states the hourly rate, and includes or links to these terms (a "Work Authorization"). Client accepts these terms by authorizing the work in writing after receiving the Work Authorization. Once accepted, these terms, as changed under Section 18, govern all services Deaver IT performs for Client until the parties enter into a Master Services Agreement. Client represents that the person authorizing work has authority to approve it on Client's behalf.

2. Scope. Deaver IT will perform the services described in the Work Authorization, in an estimate Client has approved, or in a later request for services that Deaver IT accepts. Work outside the description of a request requires Client's further authorization. These terms do not create managed services, monitoring, security services, or any ongoing obligation.

3. Rates and billing. Services are billed at the hourly rate stated in the Work Authorization, as changed under Section 18. Time is recorded in 15-minute increments. Onsite work carries a one-hour minimum per visit.

4. Travel. Travel within the Service Area is not billed. The Service Area consists of Lee, Collier, Charlotte, Hendry, Glades, Orange, Seminole, Osceola, and Lake Counties, Florida. Travel time outside the Service Area is billed at the hourly rate.

5. Estimates. Estimates are not fixed-price quotes. If work is expected to exceed an estimate by more than 20 percent, Deaver IT will notify Client and obtain Client's written approval before continuing.

6. Scheduling. Work is scheduled by mutual agreement during business hours, Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern Time, excluding holidays. These terms include no response-time commitment, no service-level agreement, and no obligation to provide after-hours or emergency support.

7. Hardware, software, and licensing. Equipment and licensing are quoted separately and billed at the time of purchase. Client is responsible for all third-party terms attached to products Deaver IT procures on its behalf.

8. Payment. Invoices are issued on completion or, for engagements exceeding two weeks, every two weeks. Payment is due 15 days from the invoice date. Undisputed amounts not paid when due accrue a late charge of 1.5 percent per month, or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less. The late charge is simple interest on the unpaid undisputed amount, calculated from its due date until paid; it does not compound and is not charged on accrued late charges. Deaver IT may require a deposit before beginning work for a client with no prior engagement history.

9. Client responsibilities. Client will provide timely access to systems, facilities, and personnel, and will designate a point of contact authorized to approve work. Client is responsible for maintaining current backups of all systems and data before work begins, and for holding all license keys, credentials, and vendor contracts required for the work.

10. Data and backups. Deaver IT will take reasonable care with Client systems and data. Client remains responsible for its own backup and recovery. Deaver IT does not monitor, manage, or verify Client backups under these terms.

11. Confidentiality. Each party will protect the other's confidential information and use it only to perform or receive the services.

12. Work product. On payment in full, Client owns the configurations and documentation created specifically for it. Deaver IT retains its own tools, templates, and methods.
